# Task 1.4.4 – History screen: pickers and By day

## Goal

The History tab shows the user's devices and the last days kept on the phone. Each day is marked Complete, Partial or No data. For the picked day it shows what happened overall and, for each reading, its Min / Average / Max and which hours have data. The 7-day summary is a later step.

## Requirements covered

- 6.1 History is read from the local database for the last 7 days, in both phases.
- 6.2 Pick a device (picking a reading comes with the 7-day summary).
- 6.3 A day-by-day strip marks each day *Complete*, *Partial* or *No data*.
- 6.4 Tap a day to see its min, average and max, and a 24-hour bar that shows which hours have no data, with a short note.
- Accessibility: colour is never the only signal; every button and spinner has a Semantics label; text scales.

Test cases: TC-1.4-002, TC-1.4-003 (device part), TC-1.4-004 to TC-1.4-007, TC-1.4-009, TC-1.4-010.

Reference: mockup v2.1 (`device-monitor-mockup-v2.1.html`, History), screens 22, 23 and 28.

## Scope

**In scope: step 1, pickers (commit 4a01033)**

- **History tab** replaces its placeholder:
  - "History" and "Last 7 days · 20–26 Oct" under it;
  - with no devices, "Add a device to see its history here." (TC-1.4-009).
- **Device picker.** One chip per device, in the home order, scrolling sideways. The picked one is filled. The first device is picked until the user picks another, or when the picked one is removed.
- **By day / 7-day summary** tabs. 7-day summary is an empty page for now. The tab says the config's number of days ("5-day summary").
- **Day picker.** It is worked out from the phone's clock and the config, with no fixed dates:
  - the last `history.daysOnPhone` local calendar days (7 for VitaCare), oldest first, today last ("Today");
  - weekday and date through `intl`, in the phone's language. The header range works across a month ("28 Oct – 3 Nov");
  - at local midnight the strip moves on. If today was picked, the new today is picked; a picked day that leaves the strip falls back to the oldest day;
  - days are local midnight to midnight, so 23 or 25 hours on a clock change.
- **Day status** (pure, `lib/domain/history/history_days.dart`):
  - a day's hours (0 = the first hour after midnight) have data when any reading was stored in them;
  - the hours that count are all of them for a past day. For today, they are the hours begun so far;
  - every hour counted has data: Complete. Some: Partial. None: No data;
  - a device added an hour ago shows today Partial and the days before No data (TC-1.4-010).
- **Legend** Complete / Partial / No data under the strip. Each tile's screen reader label says its status in words ("Thursday, October 22, No data"), or "checking data" while it loads.
- **`SegmentTabs`**: the grey two-tab switch, now shared by History and the device page's Live / History.
- **History on a device's dashboard.** Tapping History under the status strip opens the app's History tab with that device picked, By day, today. The History controller is kept alive for this. A device id that is no longer the user's falls back to their first device.

**In scope: step 2, By day detail**

- **Banner** for the picked day, under the legend. It is a live region, so screen readers hear it when another day is picked:

  | Day | Banner |
  | --- | --- |
  | No data | **No data on Thu 22 Oct** · "No readings were received from {device} on this day." |
  | Partial | **Partial data · 63% of the day** · "Missing 00:00–07:00, 22:00–24:00 (device not connected)." |
  | Today, complete so far | **Today so far** · "All readings received up to 10:42." |
  | Past day, complete | **Complete day** · "All readings received for Tue 20 Oct." |

  The percentage is the hours with data out of the hours that count, rounded down and at least 1 %, so Partial never reads 0 % or 100 %. The mockup says "readings or heartbeats"; heartbeats are kept only 24 hours and are not used, so the text says "readings".
- **One card per reading**, in the order the Live dashboard shows them (main reading, then tiles; e.g. PPG: SpO₂, Heart rate, PI (IR), PI (Red)), then any others in profile order (`historyReadings`):
  - **a normal reading:** icon, label, unit; Min / Average / Max of the day (TC-1.4-005); the **chart through the day**; and, when hours are missing, the note "No data 02:00–06:00" (TC-1.4-006);
  - **a reading with a goal** (steps, sleep, active time): the day's total (its highest value, as 1.4.3 defines it) "6,482 of 10,000 goal" and a progress bar. The profile's units for these are written for Live ("today", "last night"), so the goal line has no unit;
  - **a reading with no values that day:** "No data received this day" in a dashed box, never zeros (TC-1.4-007). On a day with no data at all every card shows it;
  - **no card:** readings worked out from events (therapy Run time, Sessions, Errors: today only), coded states (ECG rhythm), readings only drawn live (the ECG trace), and the second half of a pair (diastolic is shown with systolic).
- **Chart through the day** (requirement 6.4, mockup 22 and 28; `fl_chart`, 70 px high), from that reading's own data:
  - a line of the reading's **average in each wall-clock hour**, broken at hours without readings;
  - its normal range (profile `normal`) shaded green; the missing hours on top in one solid red (danger colour over the card, stronger in dark mode), so they look the same on every card; nothing for hours of today not begun;
  - 00:00 · 06:00 · 12:00 · 18:00 · 24:00 under it, as wall-clock times; the y-axis always takes in the normal range as well as the hourly averages (widened by 10 %), so the green band always shows and the line is seen above, in or below it (the mockup zooms to the day's values only, which hid the band on days outside the normal range); without a normal range the averages are widened by 35 %, as in the mockup;
  - not colour alone: screen readers hear "Heart rate through the day: hourly average 58 bpm to 81 bpm, no data 00:00–09:00", and the note writes the gaps out.
- **Hours:** an hour has data when at least one reading arrived in it (as the daily summaries count them). Hours are wall-clock hours: 24, or 23 when the clocks go forward; the hour repeated when they go back is one hour.
- **Loading and errors:** a labelled spinner ("Loading this day"); "Couldn't load this day." on an error. The figures are worked out again every minute, keeping the last ones on screen meanwhile.

**Out of scope**

- The 7-day summary (1.4.5), Export.
- A reading picker on By day: By day shows every reading.
- Device-specific history (therapy events, thermometer list, hub heat map) and the "activity" day rule (Used / Had errors / Not used): to agree with Nandu and QA first.
- The daily summaries themselves and the clean-up job (1.4.3, built by Dev 1).

## Data sources: the daily summaries (1.4.3)

History reads only `DailySummaryRepository` (task 1.4.3, [1.4.3-daily-summary-cleanup.md](1.4.3-daily-summary-cleanup.md)). It runs no queries of its own and changes no schema. For the chart this task adds one method to it, `hours(deviceId, day)`, which returns the hourly figures 1.4.3 already works out (`HourStats`) instead of only the day's. One `DaySummary` per device, reading and local day holds min, average, max, the count, and the wall-clock hours with readings (a bit mask).

| What History shows | From |
| --- | --- |
| Days that have ended: dots, banner, Min / Average / Max, gaps | `watch(deviceId, key, firstDay)`: the stored `daily_summaries` rows, one stream per stored reading of the profile (`storedKeys`); updated when a day is summed up, also for logs that arrive late |
| Today | `today(deviceId)`: worked out from the readings when asked; re-read every minute |
| A device's hours with data | the hour masks of all its readings combined |
| A goal reading's value | `max`, the day's total |
| The chart's hourly averages | `hours(deviceId, day)`: the day's readings added up per key and hour (sum ÷ count); re-read every minute |

**Yesterday after midnight.** The daily job runs at app start and every 24 hours, not at midnight. So History asks `refresh(from: yesterday, to: today, deviceId)` when it opens and when the day turns; otherwise a day that ended while the app was open would show No data until the next run. (Running the job at midnight would also fix this; raised with Dev 1.)

Not used by History: `heartbeats` (kept 24 hours only) and `device_events` (until the "activity" rule is agreed). Readings are not averaged in Phase 1 (decision 2026-11-13), so min / max are the real extremes.
