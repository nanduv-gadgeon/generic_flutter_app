# Task 1.2.8 – Heartbeat monitor: set interval, missed heartbeats to Offline

## Goal

The app knows whether the connected device is still being heard from. After the profile's number of missed heartbeat intervals it shows the device as **Offline**, and **Online** again when data returns. Heartbeats are kept on the phone for 24 hours.

## Requirements covered

- §12 Device heartbeat (health check): 12.1–12.3, 12.5 (Offline and Online; the banner and alert are hooks), 12.6 (Seen and Last heartbeat; error flags wait for a device that sends them), 12.7 (kept 24 h)
- §8 Device settings: heartbeat interval, last heartbeat, offline rule and device status

## Scope

**In scope**

- Profile `heartbeat` block: `intervalSeconds` (default 60), `offlineAfterMissed` (default 3), optional `setInterval` command. A profile without it uses the defaults.
- Any data from the device counts as a heartbeat: data packets, log frames, command replies and battery level. Neither shipped device has a heartbeat packet or a set-interval command (protocol docs).
- `HeartbeatMonitor` (domain) for the active device: Offline once nothing has arrived for `interval × offlineAfterMissed`, counted from when the device was opened until the first heartbeat; Online on the next one.
- `LiveHeartbeat` update and `ActiveLink.online`; home card, Connected count, device page status line and Device settings use them.
- `setInterval`, when set, is sent after every connection with the interval as its value.
- `heartbeats` table (schema v5): at most one row per interval, with the latest battery level. "Seen" (`lastHeartbeatAt`) is saved at most every 10 s from any heartbeat (it moved from the reading writer).
- The daily clean-up job also deletes heartbeats older than 24 hours.

**Out of scope**

- The offline banner and Reconnect button (1.3.6).
- The "Device offline" alert and notification (1.5.3, 1.5.4): hook at the Offline change, `TODO(1.5.3)`.
- Reconnecting by itself when Offline (decision 2026-11-11); the link's own drop-reconnect still runs.
- Heartbeat packets with status flags and uptime: added when a device first sends them.
- Watching devices that are not the active one, and background monitoring.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The connected device shows Offline on home, its page and Device settings after `offlineAfterMissed` intervals with no data, and Online again when data returns.
- [ ] Offline is said in words, never only by colour.
- [ ] Device settings shows the interval, the offline rule, the last heartbeat and the status.
- [ ] A profile's `setInterval` is sent after every connection; the config check refuses an unknown command or one without a value.
- [ ] Heartbeats are stored at most once per interval and deleted after 24 hours.
- [ ] Unit tests added (mocktail); `flutter analyze` and `flutter test` are clean
- [ ] Every new button and spinner has a Semantics label
