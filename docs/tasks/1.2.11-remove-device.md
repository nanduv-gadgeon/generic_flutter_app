# Task 1.2.11 – Remove device (swipe to delete)

## Goal

On My devices, users can delete a device by swiping its card to the left and confirming. Removing a device unpairs it from the phone and deletes its stored data.

## Requirements covered

- 3.5 **Swipe left** on a device to delete it, with a confirmation first.
- 8.5 **Remove device**: ask to confirm, unpair it and delete its stored data from the phone. *Phase 2* also tells the cloud (the existing `DeviceRegistration.unregister` hook) and stops its upload (not built yet: Phase 2).
- 3.2 The card's connection reads "BLE · Connected", "Wi‑Fi · Connected" or "Offline" (red). "BLE" was chosen in review over the requirement's and mockups' "Bluetooth".
- Accessibility: screen readers get a "Delete {name}" action on each card, and colour is never the only signal (the red panel also shows a bin icon and the word Delete).

## Scope

**In scope**

- **Full swipe** with `Dismissible`, right to left only. The red Delete panel follows the finger (`docs/screens/07_home_swipe.png`). A complete swipe opens the "Remove {name}?" bottom sheet (mockup 26):
  - **Cancel** puts the card back;
  - **Remove device** deletes it, the card leaves at once, and a snackbar says "Device removed";
  - if removal fails, the card comes back and a snackbar says so.
- The hint "Swipe left on a device to delete it." under "My devices" when there are devices.
- **Screen readers:** a custom "Delete {name}" action on each card opens the same sheet.
- **Removal, in `LocalDeviceService`:**
  1. Deletes the row. Alert limits and readings go with it (foreign-key cascade).
  2. If the removed device is the connected one, `ActiveDevice` sees it leave the list and disconnects it. This was already the case.
  3. **Unpairs a BLE device** through the new `DevicePairing` (Android `removeBond` via `universal_ble`; nothing on iOS or when not paired). A failed unpair doesn't stop the removal; the pairing can then be removed in the phone's Bluetooth settings.
  4. Tells the registration (a no-op in Phase 1).
- `removeDevice(userId, address)`, used by Add device and Device settings, now unpairs too.
- In reorder mode (1.2.10) the rows can't be swiped; the hint line shows the reorder hint instead.

**Out of scope**

- The mockup's open panel that waits for a tap on Delete: a full swipe was chosen in review.
- Undo after removing.

## Phase behaviour

| isCloud | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| false (Phase 1) | Local delete and BLE unpair only. No network calls. |
| true (Phase 2) | Also `DeviceRegistration.unregister` (DELETE /devices). The hook is already there. |

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Swiping left asks to confirm: "Remove {name}?" and "The device will be unpaired and removed from this app. You can add it again later." Swiping right does nothing.
- [x] Cancel keeps the device; Remove device deletes it and its card at once.
- [x] A failed removal keeps the card and says so.
- [x] The device's alert limits and readings are deleted. Another user's device can't be removed.
- [x] BLE devices are unpaired, Wi‑Fi devices are not; a failed unpair still removes the device.
- [x] Screen readers have a Delete action on each card.
- [x] The sheet fits a 320 × 640 phone at 2× text size.
- [x] Unit tests added (mocktail); `flutter analyze` and `flutter test` are clean.
- [ ] On a phone: a removed bonded device no longer shows in Bluetooth settings.
