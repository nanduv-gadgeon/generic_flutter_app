# Task 1.4.3 – Daily summary calculation and daily 7-day clean-up job

## Goal

Every device's readings are summed up per reading and calendar day, so History and Export can show each day's lowest, average and highest value, which hours have data, and one value per day for the 7-day chart. The daily clean-up job works these summaries out and keeps them for the same days as the readings.

## Requirements covered

- 6.3 Day strip: *Complete*, *Partial* or *No data* (the summary's hours with data).
- 6.4 Day detail: min, average and max, and the 24-hour bar of missing hours.
- 6.5 7-day summary chart: one value per day (average; for readings with a goal, the day's total).
- 6.6 Export of the last 7 days (the summaries are its per-day rows).
- 10.2 Readings older than 7 days are deleted once a day.

## Scope

**In scope**

- **Daily summary** (domain, pure): for each device, reading key and local calendar day:
  - lowest, average (over every reading, not over hours) and highest value;
  - how many readings;
  - which local hours had readings, and the share of the day with readings. A day the clocks go forward has 23 hours to fill; the hour repeated when they go back counts once.
  - For a reading with a goal (steps, sleep, active minutes), which counts up through the day, the highest value is the day's total.
- **Worked out from the database.** One query adds the readings up per device, key and hour; the domain makes days of the hours.
- **Stored for finished days only** (`daily_summaries` table, schema v7): one row per device, key and day (yyyymmdd). Today changes all the time, so it is worked out when asked for and not stored.
- **Late readings.** When a device sends readings from an earlier day (e.g. an IEF patch's logs after days away), that day's summary is worked out again as they are stored.
- **The daily job** (`ReadingCleanupJob`, at app start and every 24 h while the app runs):
  1. sums up the finished days kept: today and the `history.daysOnPhone − 1` days before it, without today;
  2. deletes summaries of older days;
  3. deletes readings and device events older than `history.daysOnPhone` days, and heartbeats older than 24 h (as before).
- Removing a device deletes its summaries.

**Out of scope**

- The History screen (1.4.4), the 7-day chart data (1.4.5) and Export (1.4.6).
- Event-based readings (IEF run time, sessions, errors): they come from `device_events`; History works their days out with `summarise()` (`lib/domain/dashboard/event_summary.dart`).
- 1-minute averages of readings older than 24 h: not done in Phase 1 (decision 2026-11-13).
- Running the job while the app is closed (workmanager / BGTaskScheduler): not done (decision 2026-11-13).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each finished day kept has one summary per device and reading with min, average, max, count and the hours with data; today has none stored but can be worked out.
- [ ] Readings stored late for an earlier day update that day's summary at once.
- [ ] The job sums up the finished days kept, deletes older summaries, and still runs its deletes when the summary step fails.
- [ ] Upgrading from schema v6 keeps all data and adds an empty `daily_summaries` table.
- [x] No new buttons or spinners (no UI in this task).
- [ ] Unit tests added (mocktail); `flutter analyze` and `flutter test` are clean.
