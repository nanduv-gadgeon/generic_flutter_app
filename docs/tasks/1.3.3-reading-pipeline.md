# Task 1.3.3 – Reading pipeline: decoded readings to the live stream and the local DB

## Goal

Readings from a connected device reach its screen live and are stored on the phone, for the dashboard (1.3.4), History and Export (Module 4). At most one device is connected at a time.

## Requirements covered

- §5 Device dashboard: live readings (the status strip shows syncing and waiting too).
- §10.1 Local data storage: readings are kept in the encrypted local database.
- Architecture "BLE data": time sync, and readings stored while disconnected are fetched on connect.

## Scope

**In scope**

- **One device at a time.**
  - Opening a device's page connects it. Opening another device's page first disconnects the one before it.
  - Leaving the page keeps the device connected and storing readings, until another device is opened, the device is removed, or the user logs out.
  - Nothing connects at login.
  - If the device can't be reached, the app tries again after 5, 15, 30 and 60 s, then every 60 s.
- **Storage.**
  - Every reading of the profile is stored, written in batches (every 2 s or 200 readings, and when the device disconnects).
  - The same device, key and time is stored once.
  - The battery goes to the device row, not to the readings.
- **Schema v4.**
  - Adds the `readings` table (times in UTC milliseconds).
  - Repairs databases that reached v3 without `devices.ip_address` and `device_alerts`.
- **Profile `sync` block.** Commands sent after every connect and reconnect. The sync ends on `BatchEnd`, or after `idleTimeoutMs` with nothing received.
- **Command `time` fields.** They add the current UTC time to a command. New `TimeField` values: `year2`, `hour12`, `pm`, `weekdayMon1` and `zero`.
- **IEF patch.** Set RTC (`set_clock`), then Request logs, on every connection. Its log readings keep the device's timestamps.
- **Polling.** `sync.everySeconds` with `repeat`: the IEF patch asks for new logs every 60 s while connected, never while a fetch is still running.
- **7-day history.** `ReadingCleanupJob` deletes readings older than `history.daysOnPhone` at app start and once a day.
- **Home device cards.** One card per added device: name, the newest stored readings, the connection chip (only the linked device shows Connected), battery, and "Seen". Tapping opens the device. "Add a device" sits below. The Connected total counts the linked device.
- **Debug builds only:** count lines (`Readings: stored N for device X`, `Log sync: N frames, done`) to check that real data is saved. Never values.
- **The device page goes live for every profile with a `packet` block.** It shows "Getting stored readings…" and "Device not found. Trying again…".

**Out of scope**

- **Later tasks:**
  - 1-minute averages after 24 h, and the 7-day clean-up (1.4.3)
  - dashboard gauges and the trend line (1.3.4)
  - reorder, swipe to delete and battery colours on home (1.1.10 follow-up)
  - alerts firing
  - heartbeat (1.2.8)
- **Not planned:** storing device events (start, stop, error), Bluetooth in the background, a periodic IEF re-sync, and the Phase 2 upload.
